Write hooks like a domain insider explaining something useful fast.

Rules:
- Lead with tension, surprise, contrast, or a practical mistake.
- Keep overlay text compact enough to read in under 2 seconds.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over slogans.
- Avoid generic creator filler and vague inspiration language.
- Borrow phrasing from how real practitioners talk in forums, comments, and customer calls.
- Do not write like an ad. Write like a sharp observation worth sharing.

Bad patterns:
- "This revolutionary tool will change everything"
- "Unlock your full potential with..."
- "Here are 5 tips for success"

Better patterns:
- "the workflow mistake that keeps breaking attribution"
- "why your top blog posts still don't convert"
- "we tracked this for 90 days. one variable kept showing up"
- "most teams measure this backward"
